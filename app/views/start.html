If you are registered with the children social care (CSC) financial oversight scheme, you can use this service to submit your financial information to the Department for Education (DfE).

About the service

The service aims to keep foster homes stable for children in care by monitoring the financial health of the most difficult to replace providers.

You will be contacted by DfE to register for the service if your organisation meets the conditions for entry.

You can read more information about the financial oversight process and entry conditions in the financial oversight scheme guidance.

What you need to provide

You must submit quarterly financial data for your organisation. You’ll be emailed a spreadsheet to record your financial information.

Upload the completed spreadsheet using this service.

DfE will review your submission and may ask you for more information.

How your data is handled and assessed

This service provides a secure way to submit your financial data. DfE uses your data to complete a financial assessment using a structured assessment criteria.

You can read the privacy notice to find out how DfE uses and stores your data.

How to access support

If you have any questions or issues regarding your submission, you can contact the customer help portal.
